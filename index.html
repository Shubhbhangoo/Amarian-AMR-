01THE PROTOCOL

CONSENSUS IS THE PRODUCT

Rules first.
Everything else follows.

Amarian turns monetary policy into deterministic state transitions. There is no privileged operator above the rules, only implementations that either satisfy them or do not.

01

Fixed issuance

Supply follows a predetermined schedule. No premine, no tail emission, no central mint authority.
